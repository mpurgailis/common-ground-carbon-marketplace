# 20%-below pricing policy

"20% below" means 20% below a competitor's final immediate-purchase checkout total for the same quantity, not 20% below a card headline.

A comparison is valid only when all of these match:

- exact registry project ID
- credit standard and methodology
- vintage, or the same disclosed vintage allocation rule
- tonne quantity
- retirement versus transfer outcome
- currency and tax treatment
- fees included in the final total
- immediate availability at both sellers

Do not substitute a project of the same category or country. Do not compare an exchange wholesale listing with a retail retirement checkout. Do not publish a price when the 20%-below target is at or below the CTX all-in acquisition cost.

Every displayed price should expose:

- project/registry ID, standard, methodology and vintage
- available tonnes and "last checked" time
- whether retirement is included
- unit price and all fees/tax before payment
- competitor benchmark link and checked time
- the exact basis for the 20% calculation

## Verified public benchmark, used for one concept price

Gold Standard's marketplace listed Vichada Climate Reforestation Project, Gold Standard project 1806, vintage 2019, at USD 40/tonne on the project page checked 16 September 2026. Search results on the same day also showed USD 42 and 2,168 units on a marketplace index page. That conflict means the final checkout must be opened and recorded immediately before using it as a benchmark.

Re-checked 16 September 2026: the product page lists USD 40.00/tonne, vintages 2019, with instant Add to cart, and the registry confirms project 1806 as a Gold Standard certified A/R project (developer Forliance). The demo storefront shows a clearly labelled concept price of USD 32.00/tonne for this project, computed as 0.8 x the listed 40.00 instant-buy price, with the benchmark listing, registry link and check date shown next to it.

There is still no verified matching CTX listing and no verified CTX all-in acquisition cost, so no real Common Ground sell price may be derived from this benchmark and the demo checkout must stay non-transactional.
